GIBSON RIDGE

Modeling.

The first stage of creating a 3D model. Every asset starts as a clean blockout where scale, proportions and silhouette are established before any detail is added.

UV Unwrapping.

Once the model is finalized, it is unwrapped into clean, distortion-free UVs. A proper UV layout is what allows textures to sit perfectly on every surface later in the pipeline.

Texturing.

Materials are authored layer by layer — color, roughness and surface detail are built up gradually until every asset reads naturally under the scene lighting.

Scene Setup.

All assets are imported into Unreal Engine, where the scene comes together — placement, lighting setup, post-process volumes and camera work define the mood of the environment.

Engine Work.

Further refinement inside Unreal Engine: shaders, foliage, particles and lighting are iterated step by step until the atmosphere matches the original vision.

Final Render.

The final stage — camera settings, exposure and post-processing come together to produce the finished rendered image of the environment.
